Onboarding flow

Role: sole designer, with one engineer and a PM. Eight weeks, 2022.

Problem

Only a third of people who signed up finished setup. Support logs showed they stalled at the team invite step because it looked required.

What I decided

We considered removing the invite step. I argued for keeping it and making it skippable, because teams that invited someone in week one retained better. The PM wanted it gone. We tested skippable first.

Outcome

Setup completion went from 34% to 58% over six weeks, measured in Amplitude. Invite rate dropped 4 points, which we accepted.
