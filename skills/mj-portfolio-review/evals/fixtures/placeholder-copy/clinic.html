Clinic booking app

My role: sole designer. Six weeks, with a volunteer developer.

The problem

People booked by phone and the line was busy most mornings. The clinic wanted bookings online, but most patients were on older Android phones on slow connections.

What I did

I ran five usability sessions on paper first. Three of five people tapped the date before the clinic, so I reordered the steps to match. I dropped a calendar widget in favor of a simple list of next available times, because the calendar was unreadable at small sizes.

What happened

The clinic has not launched it yet. If it does, we would measure the share of bookings made online and whether the morning phone queue gets shorter.
